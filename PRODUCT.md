# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Final build: Laravel + Filament admin, Blade + Tailwind + Alpine (see ofamed-rebuild-plan.md). Design pitches are plain static HTML/CSS in `pitch/`.

## Users
Hospital, clinic and pharmacy procurement staff in Indonesia (id-ID), on mobile and desktop, looking for a trustworthy medical equipment distributor and a fast way to ask for a quote (WhatsApp or form). Secondary: the client's non-technical admin.

## Product Purpose
Company profile and product catalog for PT. Ofafa Duta Medika, a medical equipment and hospital supplier / distributor. No cart or payments; success is a visitor trusting the company and sending a quote request or opening Shopee/WhatsApp.

## Positioning
Distributor with integrity, global reach and customer satisfaction as the stated priority (Responsive, Quality, Support). Own brands: Ofafa Beauty Care, Ofahusada.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Home flow to preserve: hero, intro + "Selengkapnya", 3 values, exclusive products/brands, footer with contact + online shop (Shopee, WhatsApp). Public copy in Indonesian; some English headings kept from old site. No invented prices, certifications, registration numbers, stats or testimonials; use marked placeholders. Logos, product photos and hero photo are not available yet (text wordmarks until provided). Address and phone from old site, to be confirmed.

## Brand Commitments
Red is the brand anchor (old site ~#F52F2F, white and soft pink). Name "PT. Ofafa Duta Medika" with "Duta" highlighted in the old wordmark. Keep the tagline "Medical Equipment and Hospital Supplier" and headline "Indonesia's Medical Equipment Distributor".

## Evidence on Hand
Old-site copy (ofamed-rebuild-plan.md section 5) and Wayback screenshot. Absent: logo files, photography, product list, About text.

## Product Principles
- Trust before flash: medical buyers need credibility first.
- Same story, same order as the old site, modernised.
- Never fabricate commercial or regulatory claims.
- Mobile-first and fast on Indonesian mobile networks.
